# Fitness and pilates studio management system
A database system for managing fitness and Pilates studio  daily operations. The system will help manage clients, employees, instructors, attendance, salaries, booking and payments.
Main features:
- Client management (regisrtations and profile)
- Employee managment (registration and management of instructors and employees)
- Class Management (group classes and individual training sessions)
- Schedule Management (managing instructors, rooms, and training schedules)
- Booking System (booking and cancellation of training sessions)
- Availability Checking (checking available places before booking)
- Attendance Tracking (recording client attendance)
- Payment Processing (recording and tracking payments)
- Training History (viewing clients’ previous training sessions)
- Reports (generating reports about revenue, attendance, memberships, and class popularity)
